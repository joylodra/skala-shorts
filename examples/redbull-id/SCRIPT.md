# Skala short #2: Red Bull Stratos (Bahasa Indonesia) — draft v1

## Facts (with sources)
- 14 Oct 2012, over Roswell, New Mexico: Felix Baumgartner jumped from a capsule carried by a helium balloon, exit
  altitude 38,969 m (~39 km). — FAI, Wikipedia
- Top speed 1,357.6 km/h; first person to break the sound barrier in freefall. — Guinness World Records
- Freefall a little over 4 minutes (4 min 19 s) before the parachute. — Wikipedia
- More than 8 million simultaneous live streams on YouTube at the peak, a YouTube record at the time. — YouTube/Guinness/The Star
- The capsule, suit and broadcast were branded Red Bull; Red Bull produced the event itself. — Red Bull
- NOT used: "+7% sales" (correlation only, never confirmed by Red Bull); cost estimates ($27-50 M) vary.

## Notes
- Staged as an office "watch party": the jump plays on the wall display, Skala + the team react. No real logos
  (a generic energy-drink can; the brand name only as plain text), no likeness of the real jumper (a tiny suited
  figure on the screen only).
